📖 Theory
Bohr Method — Measuring Anatomical Dead Space
1
Dead space (VD) = volume of airways where no gas exchange occurs (nose, trachea, bronchi). Normal ≈ 150 mL or ~2 mL/kg.
2
Bohr's principle: CO₂ in expired air is diluted by dead-space gas (CO₂ ≈ 0). By comparing arterial PaCO₂ (= alveolar) with mixed-expired PECO₂, we can back-calculate VD.
3
Clinical relevance: In COPD and PE, VD/VT rises above 0.35 → inefficient ventilation.
VD/VT = (PaCO₂ − PECO₂) / PaCO₂
Alveolar ventilation: VA = (VT − VD) × RR (L/min)
