📖 Theory
Ergospirometry — Exercise Energy Metabolism
1
RER = VCO₂/VO₂. Substrate indicator: 0.7 = fat; 1.0 = carbohydrates; >1.0 = lactate buffering.
2
Anaerobic threshold (AT): work rate at which lactate accumulates faster than it is removed; RER exceeds 1.0. In elite athletes, AT occurs at 80–90% VO₂max.
3
Muscle efficiency ≈ 25%: only 25% of chemical energy becomes mechanical work; 75% is heat.
RER = VCO₂ / VO₂
Energy expenditure = VO₂ × caloric equivalent (kcal/min)
Mechanical power = Total power × efficiency
VO₂max (mL/kg/min) = VO₂max (L/min) × 1000 / body mass
