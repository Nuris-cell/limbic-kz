📖 Theory
Haemodynamics — Pressure, Resistance, Cardiac Work
1
Ohm's Law for circulation: CO = ΔP/SVR. Like an electrical circuit: current = voltage / resistance.
2
SVR (dyn·s/cm⁵) = (MAP−CVP)×1333/(CO/60 mL/s). Normal: 900–1500. Falls in sepsis, rises in hypertension.
3
Fick method: CO = VO₂/(CaO₂−CvO₂). Most accurate in valvular disease.
4
LV stroke work (J/beat) = 0.0133 × MAP × SV_mL. Power = Work × HR / 60.
