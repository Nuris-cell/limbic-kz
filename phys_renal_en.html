📖 Theory
Renal Clearance — Principles and Calculations
1
Clearance of X = volume of plasma completely cleared of X per minute: C_X = (U_X × V) / P_X.
2
GFR (inulin clearance): inulin is freely filtered, not reabsorbed, not secreted → C_inulin = GFR. Normal: 90–130 mL/min.
3
If C_X > GFR → tubular secretion. If C_X < GFR → reabsorption. PAH (C_PAH ≈ 600) = renal plasma flow.
GFR = (U_inulin × V) / P_inulin (mL/min)
Fractional excretion = GFR / Renal plasma flow
Free water clearance: CH₂O = V − C_osm
