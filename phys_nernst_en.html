📖 Theory
Membrane Potential — Nernst and Goldman Equations
1
Nernst equation: E_X = (RT/zF) × ln([X]_out/[X]_in). At 37°C: RT/F ≈ 26.7 mV. For K⁺ and Na⁺ (z=+1).
2
Goldman equation: accounts for all ion permeabilities. At rest, membrane is most permeable to K⁺ → V_m closer to E_K (≈ −85 mV).
3
Hyperkalaemia (↑[K⁺]_out): E_K becomes less negative → depolarisation → arrhythmia risk. Critical: K⁺ > 6.5 mmol/L.
E_K = (RT/F) × ln([K⁺]_out / [K⁺]_in) (at T=300K: RT/F = 25.9 mV)
Driving force = V_m − E_X
🧪 Task 1 — E_K at [K⁺]ₒ = 6 mM
[K⁺]ₒ=6 mM, [K⁺]ᵢ=140 mM, T=300K (RT/F=25.9 mV). Calculate E_K.
ln(6/140) = ln(0.0429) ≈ −3.15. E_K = 25.9 × (−3.15)
