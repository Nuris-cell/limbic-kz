📖 Theory
Ion Channel Pharmacology
1
Vaughan Williams classes: Class I = Na+ blockers (lidocaine); Class II = beta-blockers; Class III = K+ blockers (amiodarone, sotalol); Class IV = Ca2+ blockers (verapamil).
2
Na+ block (Class I): reduces AP amplitude and dV/dt, slows conduction velocity, widens QRS on ECG.
3
K+ block (Class III): prolongs AP duration, lengthens QT interval. Risk of torsades de pointes when QT exceeds 500 ms.
dV/dt_max is proportional to open Na+ channels
AP duration is proportional to 1/(K+ repolarisation rate)
QTc = QT / sqrt(RR_seconds) -- Bazett formula
