Acid-Base Balance — Four Primary Disorders
1
4 disorders: Respiratory acidosis (↑CO₂), Respiratory alkalosis (↓CO₂), Metabolic acidosis (↓HCO₃⁻), Metabolic alkalosis (↑HCO₃⁻).
2
Expected compensation: Resp. acidosis → ↑HCO₃⁻ by 3.5 per 10 mmHg ↑CO₂ (chronic). Metab. acidosis → PaCO₂ = 1.5×HCO₃⁻ + 8 (Winters formula).
3
Anion gap (AG) = Na⁺ − (Cl⁻ + HCO₃⁻). Normal 8–12. ↑AG: ketones, lactate, uraemic toxins, toxins (MUDPILES).
pH = 6.1 + log₁₀(HCO₃⁻ / (0.03 × PaCO₂))
AG = Na⁺ − Cl⁻ − HCO₃⁻ (normal 8–12 mmol/L)
Winters: expected PaCO₂ = 1.5 × HCO₃⁻ + 8 ± 2
